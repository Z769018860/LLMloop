# v0.99 报告 / Report (中文 / English) — 族不相交新集合、Ultimate 见证验证、未判定程序、修复轮

## 中文
**1. 新的族不相交集合(冻结后未再调整)。** 引入此前一次都没使用过的 SV-COMP 目录 `termination-15`、`termination-restricted-15`(提交 `cdc41d5` 冻结流水线与 LLM 协议;93 个程序进入片段)。这些程序**没有无溢出标签**,所以用**交叉一致性**检验正确性:任何工具的 TRUE 与另一方的可重放溢出见证不可能同时成立。结果:我们 **91 个证明 + 1 个溢出见证(`MinusUserDefined`)= 判定 92/93**;Ultimate 83 个证明、10 个超时;CPAchecker 67;ESBMC 10;CBMC 10。配对:我们独有 9、Ultimate 独有 1(`GCD3`),精确双侧检验 **p ≈ 0.02**。**0 个矛盾**(无一个工具的 TRUE 对应一个见证,我们的证明与见证也不冲突)。这是第一次在**未参与开发**的族上明显领先 Ultimate;局限:没有真值标签(只有一致性),单次采样,翻译器/流水线是在其他数据上开发的但仍是同一研究组选的 SV-COMP 目录。
**2. Ultimate 的见证验证(更正我此前的说法)。** 用 Ultimate 生成正确性见证,再用 CPAchecker 的见证验证器(`correctness-witness-validation--overflow`)验证:52 个 TRUE 里 **51 个验证通过**、1 个 UNKNOWN(`PodelskiRybalchenko-LICS2004-Fig1`);其中 12 个是 CPAchecker 单独跑不出来、必须靠见证才通过的,**12 个全部验证成功**。因此 **Ultimate 的判定同样可独立检查**;我此前"我们的证书可独立复核是 Ultimate 做不到的"不成立。
**3. 未判定的无溢出程序及一个意外发现。** (a) 把循环不变式先试 `True`(`parity`:循环体没有任何未定义行为,采样原子反而使位运算查询变难)→ `houdini_loops` 现在先试平凡不变式,`parity` 被证明;(b) 修复提示携带验证器的**具体反例状态**后,`Benghazi` 被 1 个模型救回;(c) **基准标签错误**:`ESOP2008-easy2`(`x` 从 12 起每次加 1,`z=INT_MAX` 时溢出,最小程序本地验证会触发陷阱)与 `BrockschmidtCookFuhs-CAV2013-Fig9a` 的 SV-COMP 预期判定 `true`(无溢出)是**错的**——长时限证伪器给出了可重放见证(种子 9/13);此前"没判定"实为正确,Ultimate 对二者也超时。这提示:证伪器的单次运行 2 s 时限会漏掉需要大量迭代的违例,现已加上 25 s 长时限的第二遍。现在开发集:65 个无溢出、108 个有溢出;我们判定 **165**(61 证明 + 104 见证),Ultimate 156;我们独有 13、Ultimate 独有 4;仍未判定的无溢出程序 4 个(`AliasDarte…-Fig1`、`nestedLoop-1`、`random1d`、`FMCAD2013-Fig1`)。
**4. 修复轮。** 带具体反例的修复:开发集 6 个卡住的程序救回 1 个(`Benghazi`);新集合上 350 个证明里只有 3 个靠修复轮(2 个第 2 轮、1 个第 3 轮)——**仍然是边缘贡献**,不应作为主要贡献。
**5. 证书复核的新限制(如实披露)。** 复核器现对多循环程序给**每个循环**加契约(丢弃源码中不存在的标志变量原子)。新集合上:**单循环证书 149 个全部 VERIFIED(0 失败,6 超时)**;**多循环证书 46 个 FAILED**(76 通过,16 超时)。这些失败**全部出现在多循环程序**,我**没有查明根因**:我的推测是 CBMC 要求每个循环契约单独归纳,而我们验证器里内层循环的不变式可以依赖外层上下文(逐个删除外层原子仍失败,与此一致,但这不是证明)。佐证(非证明):涉及的 9 个程序中 8 个 Ultimate 也判 TRUE,且这些程序没有任何溢出见证。因此多循环证书的"独立复核"是**未完成**的,不能写成已验证。
**判断。** 与 Ultimate 相比:开发集 165 对 156(开发后数字,持平略高);**新的族不相交集 92/93 对 83/93(p≈0.02)**——这是目前最强的证据,但无真值标签、单次采样、仍是 SV-COMP 的同类终止性程序;Ultimate 的判定也可复核(51/52),所以"可复核"不再是差异化优势,差异在于覆盖(Ultimate 在 10 个程序上超时)。**可以更有底气地说"在这批族不相交的终止性程序上我们证明得更多",不能说"总体优于所有现有工具"。**
## English
**1. A new family-disjoint set (no tuning after the freeze).** Never-used SV-COMP directories `termination-15`, `termination-restricted-15` (pipeline and LLM protocol frozen at `cdc41d5`; 93 programs in the fragment). They carry **no no-overflow labels**, so correctness is checked by **cross-tool consistency**: a tool's TRUE and another party's replayable overflow witness cannot both hold. Result: ours **91 proofs + 1 witness (`MinusUserDefined`) = 92/93 decided**; Ultimate 83 proofs, 10 timeouts; CPAchecker 67; ESBMC 10; CBMC 10. Paired: ours-only 9 vs Ultimate-only 1 (`GCD3`), exact two-sided test **p ≈ 0.02**; **0 contradictions**. First clear lead over Ultimate on a family not used for development; caveats: no ground-truth labels, single sample, still SV-COMP-style termination programs.
**2. Validation of Ultimate's witnesses (correcting my earlier claim).** Ultimate correctness witnesses validated by CPAchecker's validator: of 52 TRUE verdicts **51 validate**, 1 UNKNOWN; in 12 programs where standalone CPAchecker cannot prove the property and needs the witness, **all 12 validate**. So **Ultimate's verdicts are independently checkable too**; my earlier "re-checkable certificates are something Ultimate lacks" was wrong.
**3. Undecided overflow-free programs and a surprise.** (a) trying the trivial invariant `True` first (`parity`: no UB in the loop body; sampled atoms only made the bit-vector queries harder) → proved; (b) repair prompts carrying the verifier's concrete counterexample state rescued `Benghazi` for one model; (c) **benchmark label errors**: SV-COMP's expected `true` (no overflow) for `ESOP2008-easy2` (x starts at 12, +1 per iteration, overflows at z = INT_MAX; a minimal program traps natively) and `BrockschmidtCookFuhs-CAV2013-Fig9a` is **wrong** — the long-timeout falsifier produced replayable witnesses (seeds 9/13); our earlier "undecided" was correct and Ultimate times out on both. The 2 s per-run limit of the first falsifier pass misses overflows needing many iterations; a 25 s second pass was added. Development set now: 65 overflow-free, 108 overflowing; we decide **165** (61 proofs + 104 witnesses), Ultimate 156; ours-only 13, Ultimate-only 4; 4 overflow-free programs remain undecided.
**4. Repair rounds.** With concrete counterexamples: 1 of 6 stuck development programs rescued; on the new set only 3 of 350 proofs needed repair — **still a marginal contribution**.
**5. New limitation of the certificate re-check (disclosed).** The re-check now puts a contract on **every** loop. On the new set: **single-loop certificates: 149 VERIFIED, 0 FAILED (6 timeouts)**; **multi-loop: 46 FAILED** (76 verified, 16 timeouts), all on multi-loop programs; **root cause not established** — my conjecture is that CBMC checks each loop contract in isolation while our verifier lets inner-loop invariants rely on the enclosing context (consistent with single outer atoms failing, but not a proof). Corroboration (not proof): for 8 of the 9 affected programs Ultimate also answers TRUE, and none has an overflow witness. Multi-loop independent re-checking is therefore **incomplete**.
**Judgement.** Development set 165 vs 156 (post-development, tie to slight lead); **new family-disjoint set 92/93 vs 83/93 (p ≈ 0.02)** — the strongest evidence so far, but unlabelled, single-sample, same kind of SV-COMP termination programs; Ultimate's verdicts are also checkable (51/52), so checkability is no longer a differentiator, coverage is (Ultimate times out on 10). **We can say we prove more on this family-disjoint set; we cannot say we are better than all existing tools overall.**
